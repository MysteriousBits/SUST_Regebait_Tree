\codeWithDescription{Min Cost Max Flow}{flow_matching/mcmf.cpp}%
[Build graph with \texttt{add\_edge()}. \texttt{min\_cost\_flow()} returns \texttt{\{max\_flow, min\_cost\}}. Costs can be negative/zero. No negative cycles are allowed.
\texttt{flow(edge\_id)} returns flow through an edge, where id is 0-based index of edges as they were added.\\
\textbf{Time:} {$O(VE + F E \log V)$} where $F$ is max flow.]

\codeWithDescription{HopcroftKarp}{flow_matching/hopcroft.cpp}
[Fast bipartite matching. \texttt{n} and \texttt{m} are size of left and right partitions. \texttt{g} is the \textbf{1 based indexed} adjacency list of the left partition. \texttt{l} is a vector of 0s of size \texttt{n + 1};
after the call, \texttt{l[i]} is the right-side match of left vertex $i$, or 0 if unmatched. Returns the size of the maximum matching. \\
\textbf{Time:} {$O(E\sqrt{V})$}]
